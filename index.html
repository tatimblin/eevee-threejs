eevee-threejs

A small, asset-free reimplementation of Blender/Eevee's lighting + view-transform pipeline (sun + gradient-sky world + Shader to RGB diffuse + Filmic/AgX) for Three.js custom shaders.

Lit Primitives → Sphere + torus-knot lit by the module. Switch view transforms live. Painterly Cloud → Billboard cloud with a procedurally-generated brush atlas.
